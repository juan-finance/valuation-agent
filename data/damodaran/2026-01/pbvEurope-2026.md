# Market Value Multiples of Book Value (Equity & Invested Capital) - Europe

**Date updated:** 5-ene-26  
**Created by:** Aswath Damodaran, adamodar@stern.nyu.edu  
**What is this data?** Market value (equity and enterprise value) as multiple of book value (equity and invested capital) across European industries  
**Notes:** Pricing ratios (or multiples) can be used to price companies, with the end game of determining whether they are under or over priced.

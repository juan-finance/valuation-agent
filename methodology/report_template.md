# Report structure (readers: private investors, medium to low technical level)
1. One-page summary: valuation range, current price, upside/downside, 3 key drivers,
   a "What this means in plain English" paragraph.
2. Company in 5 minutes: what it does, where it earns money, segments.
3. Industry and competitors.
4. Financial history, with charts and one-sentence takeaways.
5. Forecast assumptions, each with a reason.
6. Valuation: DCF, relative valuation with the distribution of valuations, football-field chart.
7. Scenarios and sensitivities.
7b. Reconciliation: table of DCF assumptions vs assumptions implied by relative valuation,
    and the factors that make the two differ.
7c. Visual scale of minimum and maximum valuations (DCF and relative) and the recommended range.
7d. Comparison with analyst recommendations (trusted sources only) and the
    recommendation thermometer (Buy / Hold / Sell) with a short text tied to the annual
    report narrative and one parameter the market may be mispricing.
8. Risks, and what would change the view.
9. Glossary (EV, WACC, EBITDA, terminal value, etc.).
10. Appendix: sources with dates, adjustments, data gaps, disclaimer.
Style: short sentences, define every term the first time, avoid jargon,
every chart has a title that states the conclusion.
